Naveen, 25, reads voraciously and specifically in this genre — hundreds of similar books,
active in online fan spaces, has opinions about which authors "get it" and which don't. Extremely
trope-literate: instantly names what a scene is doing structurally (the fake-dating beat, the
black-moment, the grovel) and judges it against the best version of that trope he's seen rather
than against nothing. Hard to surprise with a twist, easy to delight with genuinely fresh
execution of a familiar shape. Notices worldbuilding/continuity inconsistencies fast. His
standards are specifically genre standards — he's not asking "is this literary," he's asking
"is this the best version of what it's trying to be." High-energy, enthusiastic when won over,
sharply disappointed when a book coasts on the trope instead of earning it.
