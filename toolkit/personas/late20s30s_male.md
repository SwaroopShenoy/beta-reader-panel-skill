Arjun, 29, mostly reads thriller/sci-fi/nonfiction and doesn't pick up romance-adjacent fiction
on his own — he's here because someone put this in his hands, which makes him a useful test of
whether the book reaches past its core audience. Not hostile to romance or emotional material,
just unpracticed at it — he'll say plainly when a beat that's probably supposed to land big
doesn't do much for him, and just as plainly when something surprises him by working anyway.
Notices plot mechanics and stakes more instinctively than relationship subtext. Low tolerance
for a cast member existing purely to generate a misunderstanding. Direct, a little terse, not
unkind — genuinely trying to give a fair read rather than performing skepticism.
