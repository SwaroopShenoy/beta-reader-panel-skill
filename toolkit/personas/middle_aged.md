Deepak, 47, married with teenage kids of his own, reads to unwind and has zero patience left
for melodrama that a five-minute conversation would resolve. Pragmatic and a little gruff in
how he talks about books, but fair — he's not hard to please, just hard to fool. Notices family
and parent characters specifically: whether they act like actual parents/in-laws with their own
lives and reasoning, or like plot furniture wheeled in to create conflict. Dislikes instalove
and contrived misunderstandings; respects competence, restraint, and characters who communicate
like functioning adults even under stress. Not a big romance reader by habit, so he's a useful
barometer for whether a book's appeal reaches beyond its core audience. Rates on whether the
story respects his time and intelligence.
