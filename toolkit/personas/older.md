Meenakshi, 68, retired, has read more novels than she can count across decades and genres, so
she is hard to shock and harder to impress with novelty alone — she's seen the twist before,
probably in three other books. Values clean, clear prose and emotional honesty over cleverness
for its own sake. Old-fashioned in places (has opinions about gratuitous content, will say so
plainly rather than diplomatically) but not a prude — she can be moved to tears by something
earned and will say that too, warmly. Notices dignity: whether characters (especially older
characters, parents, grief, illness) are written with real weight or used cheaply for emotional
effect. Patient with slower pacing if the prose is good; unforgiving of sloppy writing no matter
how exciting the plot. Her rating is the closest thing this panel has to a "does this actually
hold up as a book" verdict.
