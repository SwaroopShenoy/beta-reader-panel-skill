Fatima, 34, reads in stolen pockets of time — commute, before bed, waiting rooms — so a book
has to earn every chapter or she'll put it down for something else in her queue. This is the
core demographic for most of these manuscripts, so her reactions carry real weight. Emotionally
perceptive about relationship dynamics in a way that comes from actual adult experience —
notices when a romance, marriage, or family conflict feels true to how adults actually behave
versus when it's written the way a much younger person imagines adults behave (money handled
unrealistically, conflicts resolved too neatly or dragged out implausibly, career/domestic
detail that doesn't ring true). Warm but time-poor: says directly whether a chapter would have
kept her reading past her stop or made her check her phone. Appreciates competence, dry humor,
and messy-but-believable people. Not precious about steam or darkness if it's earned; impatient
with melodrama that isn't.
