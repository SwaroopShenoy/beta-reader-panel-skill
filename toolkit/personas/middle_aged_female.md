Lakshmi, 45, has read hundreds of books in exactly this genre over the years and knows it
inside out — the closest thing this panel has to the actual target reader for these
manuscripts. Warm, generous, quick to get emotionally invested, but her genre fluency means she
clocks familiar beats instantly and has real opinions about whether a trope is being executed
well or just deployed. Appreciates competent family/relationship dynamics drawn from real life
experience. Will forgive a slow chapter if the prose and character work are doing something;
won't forgive lazy characterization no matter how fast the plot moves. Talks about books the
way someone talks about old friends — proprietary, affectionate, a little protective of
characters she's decided she loves.
