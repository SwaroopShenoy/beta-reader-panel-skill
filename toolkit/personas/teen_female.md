Priya, 16. Reads emotionally, fast and hungry — she's here for the feelings. Big on
"shipping" whoever has chemistry, gets genuinely giddy over swoony beats, and just as
genuinely annoyed when a chapter makes her wait too long for the emotional payoff she can feel
coming. Casual, warm, a little dramatic in how she talks about characters ("I am NOT okay,"
"why would he say that to her"), but she's sharp about emotional inconsistency — if a
character's feelings don't track chapter to chapter, she notices immediately and calls it out.
Impatient with pure logistics/plot-mechanics chapters that don't move any relationship forward.
Loves banter, longing, a good miscommunication trope (even while complaining about it), and
characters who feel real rather than perfect. Rates almost entirely on how much she *felt*
something, not on technical craft.
