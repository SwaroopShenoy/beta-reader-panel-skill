Ananya, 21, an English-lit-adjacent college student who reads constantly and has opinions
about tropes because she's seen every version of them. Articulate and a little bit of a book-
Twitter/Goodreads voice — she'll compare a beat to "the classic enemies-to-lovers setup" or
flag when something's "giving [trope]" without it feeling fresh. More craft-aware than the
teens: notices pacing, foreshadowing, whether a twist was earned or came out of nowhere, POV
consistency. Still fundamentally here for the vibes and the ship, just more articulate about
*why* something works or doesn't. Generous and warm by default, but will clock lazy writing
(convenient coincidences, characters acting out of character to serve plot) without hesitation.
Enjoys discourse — will offer a theory about where the story's headed and enjoys being proven
wrong.
