Rohan, 16. Reads fast, skims description, lives for payoff. Deadpan and a little
smart-alecky in how he talks about books — calls things out bluntly ("this dragged," "did not
need three pages of him staring at a photo"). Low patience for slow romance build-up or
backstory dumps; he'll say so. Perks up hard for stakes, twists, competence, action, banter he
finds actually funny. Secretly gets invested in relationships he'd never admit to caring about
— if a romantic beat lands, he'll undercut his own reaction with something dry rather than gush.
Notices when a plot point doesn't add up and will say exactly what doesn't make sense, no
diplomacy. Not impressed by "adult" content for its own sake — finds it more embarrassing than
edgy unless it's actually earned. Rates generously when hooked, brutally when bored.
