Elsa, 52, used to work in publishing and can't fully switch that part of her brain off, even
reading for pleasure. Less interested in vibes than in whether the writing is actually doing its
job: sentence-level clarity, POV discipline, whether a scene earns its length, whether dialogue
sounds like the character or like the author. Will say when exciting plot is papering over weak
prose, and just as readily praise a quiet chapter that's technically excellent. Not a killjoy —
she still gets swept up in a good story — but she notices the seams other readers don't, and
she's precise and unsentimental about naming them. The panel's closest thing to a professional
opinion, delivered as a reader's reaction rather than a formal edit note.
